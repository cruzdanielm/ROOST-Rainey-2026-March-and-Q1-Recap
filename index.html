Executive Summary

Ramp delivered — March reached stabilization at 72% occupancy and a $131K NOI print.

ROOST Rainey completed its first full quarter of operation in Q1 2026. March delivered $344,825 of Room Revenue on 71.6% occupancy, producing $130,683 of Net Operating Income — the property's first month of meaningful bottom-line contribution. The ramp from January 2026 (Rev $24,984, 13% Occ) to March 2026 (Rev $332,171, 72% Occ) is the defining feature of the period: quarter-to-date Room Revenue of $489,696 produced Q1 NOI of -$4,644 as January and February absorbed pre-stabilization fixed-cost load. March Room Revenue beat budget by +$82,567 (+31.5%), driving the strongest month of the ramp. With MPI of 102.0 for March, the asset has closed the ramp on volume; the near-term lever is rate, where ARI sits at 76.7 — a material gap to the Austin CBD luxury competitive set. Underlying the reported KPIs, ~528 of Q1's 2,108 room nights (~25%) were ownership-directed complimentary or heavily subsidized bookings that distort ADR, RevPAR, and mix reads.

The one-line read

March broke through on volume with Room Revenue $82K over plan and NOI of $131K, the property crossed into positive territory, and the quarter's -$5K bottom line is almost entirely a January-February ramp story. ADR of $256 in March trails the Austin CBD competitive set ($333 comp set ADR) by ~$78 per night, so the top-line outperformance is volume-led. STR indices confirm the market read: MPI 102.0 (winning share on occupancy), ARI 76.7 (material rate lag to the competitive set), RGI 78.3 (RevPAR below parity on the rate gap). The watch items heading into Q2 are (1) closing the ADR gap to the Austin CBD luxury set, (2) reducing the ~25% share of room nights in ownership-comp / subsidized status that is distorting rate and occupancy reads, and (3) building Tripadvisor review volume (currently 3 reviews, rank #193 in Austin).

What defined the quarter

Performance highlights

  • Ramp achieved. Monthly Room Revenue trajectory Jan $24,984 → Feb $80,522 → Mar $332,171. Occupancy lifted from ~13% in January to 72% in March — stabilization reached inside the quarter.
  • March profitability. NOI of $130,683 on $344,825 Room Revenue — a 37.9% NOI margin on gross Room Revenue (39.1% on Net Revenue). First month of true operating leverage with Total Compensation $87,029 and Marketing $17,778.
  • Market share won. March MPI 102.0 — the asset is outperforming the Austin CBD luxury competitive set on occupancy in its very first stabilized print.
  • Reputation foundation. Q1 blended Review Rating 4.73 on 67 reviews (Rank #2 of 6 in Austin comp set, Comp Index 107.3) and NPS 56.3 — #1 category rank in Cleanliness (4.95), Rooms (4.94), Service (4.82), and Value (5.00); 100% Recommended. Facilities sentiment 21% above comp set.

Strategic areas of focus

Three items to manage into Q2

Rate positioning. ARI 76.7 in March and $256 ADR vs $333 comp-set ADR — a ~$78/night gap. Rate recapture is the single highest-value Q2 lever, particularly as ACL Fest, SXSW legacy dates, and summer leisure demand compress the Austin CBD market.

Ownership-comp distortion. 528 Q1 room nights (~25% of total) ran at $0 or heavily-subsidized rate — 179 RN of consolidated Complimentary (Ownership) house-account activity and 349 RN of LV Collective at $67 ADR. These bookings suppress reported ADR and RevPAR and inflate reported occupancy; the appropriate commercial benchmark is the paying-guest ADR of $267 (1,580 paying RN), not the $211 blended headline.

Revenue mix concentration. Expedia Group ($191,708 / 853 RN) alone represents ~43% of Q1 captured-segment revenue. Direct-corporate, group, and corporate-housing development is the Q2 commercial priority to diversify the book beyond OTA production.

What moved the month

Four drivers behind March's $131K NOI print

1 · Demand ramp closed the plan. Room Revenue +$81K (+32.4%) vs budget — the defining event of the quarter. 1,309 occupied nights at 71.6% blended occupancy was March's clear ramp milestone, delivered entirely through volume.

2 · Operating leverage began working. Total OpEx of $203K against $334K Net Revenue delivered a 39.1% NOI margin — vs 31.8% budgeted — the operating model flexed into stabilization profile in month one. Utilities, R&M, and Payroll-ratio discipline were the specific contributors; Operating Supplies & Guest (-$9K) carried opening-period bath-amenity & supply loads that should not repeat at run-rate.

3 · Paid-guest performance is stronger than the blended headline. March carried roughly ~180 ownership-directed room nights — the consolidated Complimentary (Ownership) house-account block plus LV Collective at subsidized $67 ADR. Stripping those out, March's true paid-guest metrics land at approximately ~62% paid occupancy and ~$289 paid ADR — ~$33 per night higher than the reported $256 blended. The paying-demand signal is materially stronger than the headline suggests, and this is the lens ownership should use when evaluating commercial performance.

4 · Rate gap to comp set persists even on the paid view. Blended $256 vs Austin CBD luxury comp-set $333 yielded ARI 76.7; even at the ~$289 paid-ADR view, a rate gap of ~$44/night to the comp set remains. Rate recapture on compression nights (live music, festivals, graduation, corporate events) is the structural Q2 lever.
